# Brew methods

| Method | Grind | Time | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Espresso | Fine | 25-30 s | Intense, syrupy |
| Pour-over | Medium | 2.5-3.5 min | Clean, bright |
| French press | Coarse | 4 min | Full, rich |
| Cold brew | Coarse | 12-18 h | Smooth, sweet |

::::{tab-set}
:::{tab-item} Pour-over
Rinse the filter, add coffee, bloom with twice its weight in water for 30 seconds, then pour in slow circles.
:::
:::{tab-item} French press
Add coarse coffee and hot water, wait four minutes, press gently and pour right away.
:::
:::{tab-item} Cold brew
Steep coarse coffee in cold water in the fridge overnight, then strain. See {doc}`ratios` for amounts.
:::
::::
