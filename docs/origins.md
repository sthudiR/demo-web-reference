# Origins

Coffee grows in the "bean belt" between the tropics. Here are four well-known regions.

::::{grid} 1 1 2 2
:gutter: 3

:::{grid-item-card} Ethiopia
Birthplace of Arabica. Floral, tea-like and citrusy cups; often washed or natural processed.
:::
:::{grid-item-card} Colombia
Balanced and sweet, with caramel and red fruit. Two harvests a year keep supply steady.
:::
:::{grid-item-card} Brazil
The largest producer. Nutty, chocolatey and low in acidity; a classic espresso base.
:::
:::{grid-item-card} Indonesia
Earthy, heavy-bodied cups from Sumatra, often wet-hulled (see {term}`Washed`).
:::
::::
